Short-form content workflow

Create. Review. Publish.

CineShorts Studio is a desktop content creation workflow designed to help creators prepare, review and publish their own original short-form video content.

The workflow combines content preparation, video processing, quality review and authorized publishing tools in one streamlined process.

How it works

Create

Original short-form videos are prepared through a structured content production workflow.

Review

Videos and publishing information can be reviewed before they are sent to supported platforms.

Publish

Authorized creators can send their own content to supported platforms through official platform integrations.
