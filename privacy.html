Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CineShorts Studio handles information when creators use the application and authorize supported third-party platform integrations.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, CineShorts Studio may process information necessary to provide its content preparation and publishing functionality, including:

  • Basic account information provided by an authorized platform.
  • Authorization credentials and access tokens issued by a platform.
  • Videos and associated publishing information selected by the user.
  • Technical information required to complete authorized API requests.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide functionality requested by the user, such as authenticating an account, preparing content and sending user-selected videos to supported platforms.

CineShorts Studio does not use platform authorization to publish content without an action or approval from the authorized user.

3. Platform Authorization

CineShorts Studio uses official authorization mechanisms provided by supported third-party platforms.

Users authenticate directly with the applicable platform. CineShorts Studio does not request or store the user's third-party account password.

4. Access Tokens

Access tokens may be stored locally when necessary to maintain an authorized connection to a supported platform.

Tokens are used only for the permissions granted by the user and should not be publicly exposed or shared.

5. User Content

Videos, images, captions and other content remain under the control and responsibility of the user.

Content selected for publishing may be transmitted to the destination platform as necessary to perform the requested upload or publishing operation.

6. Sharing of Information

CineShorts Studio does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a supported third-party platform when necessary to perform an operation explicitly requested or authorized by the user.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored authorization information may be retained for as long as necessary to maintain the user's authorized connection.

Users may revoke authorization through the applicable third-party platform. Local authorization data may also be removed when the integration is disconnected.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored authorization information and prevent unauthorized access. Users should also protect access to the device on which CineShorts Studio is operated.

9. Third-Party Services

Third-party platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms. CineShorts Studio does not control the privacy practices of those services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CineShorts Studio evolves. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the operator of CineShorts Studio through the contact information associated with the application.
